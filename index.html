    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How does the free quote work?

    Book with the form above or call or text 647-388-8488. We reach you at the time you choose, talk through the project and give you a free, clear quote.

    What areas do you serve?

    Forest Hill and the surrounding areas of Toronto. If you’re nearby and not sure, ask: book a quote and tell us your street.

    How long does a job take?

    It depends on the size of the job and the weather. We’ll tell you how long we expect to be on site when we give you your quote.

    How does prep work?

    Prep comes first, because it’s what makes the paint last: cleaning, scraping, sanding and taping so the new coat bonds and holds up, with floors, furniture and plants protected.

    What paint do you use?

    It depends on the surface: siding, doors, decks and interior walls each need something different. We’ll go through the options with you when we give your quote.

    Do I need to be home?

    We’ll sort this out when we book. For interior work someone needs to let us in; for exterior work we just need to be able to reach the areas being painted.

    What happens if it rains?

    Exterior paint and stain need dry weather, so rain can move an exterior job. We’ll keep you posted and agree the new day with you.
