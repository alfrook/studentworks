Meet Alexander Frook

Owner/Operator, Student Works Painting

This coming season, I'll be running the Student Works Painting division in Forest Hill! I'm a graduate of Forest Hill Collegiate Institute and I'm headed into my second year of mechanical engineering at the University of Guelph. I'll be devoting my season to painting homes and servicing past, present and future clients, possibly you! I'm bringing plenty of painting experience from across Grey-Bruce to our neighborhood.

It's my goal to run one of the top Student Works divisions in the province this year. Running a successful painting business means combining a large number of projects with top levels of service, quality and customer satisfaction, and that's exactly what I'm aiming for.

No matter what type of painting or staining project you have in mind, residential or commercial, we can help with a crew you can count on in the Forest Hill area.
