Owner/Operator, Student Works Painting

This coming season, I'll be running the Student Works Painting division in Forest Hill! I'll be devoting my season to painting homes and servicing past, present and future clients, possibly you! I'm bringing plenty of painting experience from across Grey-Bruce to our neighborhood.
