About Student Works

Student Works has been painting homes across Canada since 1981, with interior and exterior painting for hundreds of thousands of homeowners. Every summer it recruits and trains motivated university and college students to run their own painting businesses and pay for their education.

More than 5,000 homes are painted each year, with a written 3-year guarantee and thorough prep work behind every job. The company's goal is to create leaders as much as it is to paint houses.

Every job is backed by a written 3-year guarantee and liability coverage, so your home and property are protected from start to finish. Service quality and customer satisfaction are our guarantees. We hire for attitude, not experience, and every job starts with a free estimate.

Our crew is led by Alexander Frook.
