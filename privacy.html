Privacy notice

How we handle your details.

Short and plain: we only use what you send us to get back to you about your painting project. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Who we are

ForestHill Student Painters, a student-run house painting business serving Forest Hill and the surrounding areas of Toronto, Ontario. The business is run by Alexander Frook. You can reach us at 647-388-8488 or paintwithfrook@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the booking form: your name, how to reach you (a phone number or an email address), the best time to call (if you chose a call), what needs painting, and when you would like it done. We don’t ask for anything else and we don’t collect payment details on this website.

How it gets to us

When you press “Book my free quote”, your answers are sent by email to us through FormSubmit, a service that delivers website forms by email. We don’t keep them in a database on this website: there isn’t one.

What we use it for

To contact you by phone, text or email about your quote, and to schedule it. We may text or call the number you gave, or email the address you gave. We don’t sell your details or share them with anyone else for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and has no analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google’s servers receive your device’s address and browser details when a page loads, as they do for any site that uses it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them at any time. Call or email us (above) and we’ll deal with it.

Changes

If we change how we handle your details, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
